Between the last of the work
and the first of the dark
an hour comes that asks nothing,

that stands at the field gate
with its hands in its pockets
and says nothing worth repeating.

The kettle. The chair by the window.
The dog turning three times
before she settles.

Somewhere a gate swings.
Somewhere a car goes by
on a road that is not this one.

I have spent my life
trying to be somewhere,
and here is somewhere, arriving

without being sent for,
the way the light does,
the way the evening does.
