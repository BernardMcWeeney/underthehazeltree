The leaves hold whispers
the branches understand,
and light comes down
in gentle hands.

Not all at once —
it has learned patience
from the slow work
of a hundred summers.

It comes in coins,
in shillings of gold
spent on the path
and the path spends them back.

I stood there long enough
to be counted among the trees,
long enough that a wren
mistook me for a quiet thing

and went about her business
in the hedge beside my ear,
and I have not been paid
a finer compliment since.
