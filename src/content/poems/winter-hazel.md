Bare, the tree shows its working:
every decision it ever made
about the light,

each turn away from the wall,
each reaching toward the gap
where the sun would be at four.

In summer it hid all this
under a green agreeableness,
the way we do.

Now the whole argument stands
against a low white sky
and I find it beautiful —

not in spite of the plainness.
Because of it. Because
nothing is being claimed.

The catkins are already there,
small and stubborn as commas
in a sentence not yet written.
